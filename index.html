Navigate Relationship Import App

Upload a student list, review it for duplicates and problems, and export a Navigate relationship import file that assigns or removes advisors, mentors, and other staff relationships.

Version:1.0 Released:September 15, 2026 Developed by:Josh Goldblatt using Claude

Step 1 — Upload

Accepts .xlsx, .xls, or .csv. Required columns: Student ID and Relationship Type. Optional: Staff ID and Action (add/delete). Extra columns are ignored.

Navigate staff ID of the advisor, mentor, or director being assigned or removed.
